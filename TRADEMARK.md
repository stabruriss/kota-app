## Allowed Uses

You may:

- refer to the Kota project by name;
- say that software works with Kota or is compatible with Kota, if true;
- link to the official Kota website or repository;
- make truthful, non-misleading statements about the origin of the source code;
- reproduce required notices from the Apache-2.0 `NOTICE` file;
- use Kota feature and concept names descriptively and truthfully, including
  names that are common words or that coincide with third-party projects.

## Not Allowed

You may not, without permission:

- use "Kota" as the name of an unofficial product, fork, service, package, or
  app, or use a confusingly similar name, package name, app name, domain, logo,
  or visual identity;
- use Kota logos, icons, official visual assets, or official brand identity;
- use Kota official UI/UX, copy, artwork, or brand expression in a way that
  suggests official status, endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation;
- imply that an unofficial fork, distribution, service, or product is the
  official Kota distribution.

## Forks And Distributions

Forks, modified distributions, hosted services, and packages based on Kota must
use a different name and must not imply they are the official Kota distribution.

Descriptive phrases such as "based on Kota" or "compatible with Kota" are
allowed when they are truthful and do not imply official status, endorsement,
sponsorship, or affiliation.
