# Trademark Policy

Kota(TM) is a trademark and brand identifier of Nan Wu.

This policy is intended to let people accurately refer to Kota while preventing
confusing or misleading uses of Kota names, marks, and official identity.

## Official Names And Marks

Official Kota names and marks include Kota, Kota logos, official Kota visual
identity, and official product or system names used by the project, including
Tavern, Dreams, Source Hero, Incarnation, Violet, Ember, Bartender, Magi,
Laughing Man, BBS, and Puppeteer.

This list may be updated as the project evolves. These names and marks are not
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

This trademark policy does not restrict functional use of these names as
identifiers inside source code that is licensed under Apache-2.0. It restricts
using them to identify the source, sponsorship, affiliation, or official status
of your own product, service, package, fork, or distribution.

## Allowed Uses

You may:

- refer to the Kota project by name;
- say that software works with Kota or is compatible with Kota, if true;
- link to the official Kota website or repository;
- make truthful, non-misleading statements about the origin of the source code;
- reproduce required notices from the Apache-2.0 `NOTICE` file.

## Not Allowed

You may not, without permission:

- use Kota as the name of an unofficial product, fork, service, package, or app;
- use Kota logos, icons, official visual assets, or official brand identity;
- use official Kota system names or marks in a way that suggests official
  status, endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation;
- use a confusingly similar name, mark, package name, app name, domain, logo, or
  visual identity;
- imply that an unofficial fork, distribution, service, or product is the
  official Kota distribution.

## Forks And Distributions

Forks, modified distributions, hosted services, and packages based on Kota must
use a different name and must not imply they are the official Kota distribution.

Descriptive phrases such as "based on Kota" or "compatible with Kota" are
allowed when they are truthful and do not imply official status, endorsement,
sponsorship, or affiliation.

## Contact

For non-security brand-use questions, open an issue in this repository. Do not
include confidential information in public issues.
